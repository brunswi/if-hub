# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

_provenance: inferred — basis: the hub copy addresses editors ("Ihre Arbeit im IF-CMS"), programmers and agencies ("Programmierer und Agenturen") of Sparkasse websites.

Editors, web teams and partner agencies of German Sparkassen who maintain the Sparkasse "Internet-Filiale" (the savings banks' public websites). Access is internal: anonymous visitors land on a login screen ("Interne Benutzeranmeldung").

## Product Purpose

IF-Hub is the knowledge and guideline portal for the Internet-Filiale: news, editorial rules (Redaktionelles Regelwerk), technical guidelines, a podcast, support contacts and links to related services (App-Shop, Media-Center, UI-Guidelines, Schulungsprogramm, Content-Service, Info-Mail-Datenbank). It runs on the Frontify brand-portal platform under the DSGV Markenportal.

## Positioning

"Herzlich willkommen im IF-Hub — News, Tipps und Anleitungen rund um Ihre Internet-Filiale": one place to keep a Sparkasse's online presence current, customer-oriented and legally compliant.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Login gate on / (SSO button "Anmelden über SSO", "Zugang beantragen" link).
- Terms-and-conditions acceptance dialog before hub content is shown.
- Background video hero, image tiles linking to guideline sections, search in the header.

## Brand Commitments

- Register: brand (internal marketing/portal surface).
- Personality: institutional, friendly, clear — Sparkasse red (#ee0000) header and buttons, white space, pill buttons, Sparkasse corporate typefaces.
- Anti-references (observed): no dense dashboards, no decorative gradients or shadows.
